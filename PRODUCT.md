<h1 align="center">What is MezoChat?</h1>

<p align="center"><b>A Bitcoin-backed bank account that lives inside Telegram.</b></p>

---

## The idea

Bitcoin is the strongest savings asset in the world, but day-to-day spending runs on dollars. Mezo connects the two with **MUSD**, a dollar stablecoin you borrow against your Bitcoin. Your Bitcoin stays yours, and you get dollars to spend.

**MezoChat brings MUSD to people where they already are: their chats.** Over a billion people use Telegram every month. With MezoChat, borrowing, paying, splitting bills and shopping become as easy as sending a message.

> Hold Bitcoin. Spend dollars. Do it all in a chat.

## Who it's for

- **Bitcoin holders** who want spending money without selling their BTC.
- **Friends and families** who split rent, dinners and trips and want instant, transparent settlement.
- **Freelancers and small teams** who want to send and request payments by @username.
- **Newcomers to crypto** who want a friendly first wallet with a PIN, in an app they already know.

## What you can do

| | |
|---|---|
| **Own a wallet in seconds** | Create or import a wallet right inside Telegram. It's protected by your PIN and encrypted on your device. MetaMask, Trust, Coinbase and OKX connect too. |
| **Borrow MUSD against Bitcoin** | Deposit BTC and borrow MUSD with a live quote from Mezo's contracts, including your ratio, liquidation price and fees, all before you sign. |
| **Manage your loan** | Add BTC, repay MUSD or close your loan and take your BTC back, with every change previewed first. |
| **Pay anyone by @username** | `/send 25 to @alice`. Your payment settles on Mezo, and both of you get a receipt in chat. |
| **Request money and split bills** | `/split 120 with @alice @bob`. Everyone gets a Pay button, and you see who has paid. |
| **Automate payments** | `/pay 800 to @landlord on the 1st if ratio > 160`. Rent goes out on time while your Bitcoin stays protected. |
| **Shop with MUSD** | Browse Mezo Market inside Telegram, with live prices and stock, and buy gift cards with brand codes delivered in chat. |
| **Stay safe automatically** | MezoChat watches your loan and alerts you before your collateral gets close to your safety level. |
| **Just talk** | *"send bob twenty for lunch"* works. MezoChat understands everyday language. |

## Why it matters

**1. Distribution.** The best financial product is the one people actually open. Telegram puts MUSD one tap away for a huge, global, crypto-native audience, with no new app to download.

**2. Safety by design.** Every spend checks your live collateral ratio first. Recurring payments wait while your ratio is below your chosen level. Your Bitcoin is protected by default.

**3. Real ownership.** MezoChat is non-custodial. Your key is encrypted on your device with your PIN, and only you can sign.

**4. Real utility for MUSD.** Borrowing, payments, splits, subscriptions and shopping make MUSD a currency people use every day.

## How a typical day looks

1. **Morning:** Ada opens Telegram and sees "✓ Rent paid to @landlord, ratio 182%".
2. **Lunch:** she types *"split 45 with @tolu @mike"*. Both friends get a Pay button and settle in seconds.
3. **Afternoon:** BTC dips. MezoChat pings her: "Your ratio is 158%. Add BTC or repay to stay safe." She taps **Add BTC** and is back to 190%.
4. **Evening:** she buys a gift card with MUSD, and the code lands in her chat.

She never left Telegram, and she never sold her Bitcoin.

## Built on Mezo

MezoChat runs directly on Mezo's contracts: **MUSD**, **TroveManager**, **BorrowerOperations**, **PriceFeed** and the **Mezo Store**. It adds its own on-chain **HandleRegistry**, so every @handle is verifiable on Mezo. Every number you see (prices, ratios, fees, stock and gas) is read live from the chain.
